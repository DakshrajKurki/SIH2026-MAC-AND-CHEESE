  adversarial search batteries.

  sample counts fall below N < 30 continuous inference events due to asymptotic sample limitations
  in empirical Wasserstein and Kolmogorov-Smirnov distance formulations.

  pre-capture cannot be prevented by digital hashing alone.

  annotation topologies for training data, and ONNX, PyTorch State-Dict, and TensorRT engine
